Working Style

My delivery process is designed to reduce risk early.

  1. ClarifyUnderstand the business goal, user flow, scope, and constraints.
  2. InspectReview codebase, architecture, dependencies, builds, logs, and release process.
  3. PrioritizeSeparate release blockers, crashes, performance problems, cleanup, and feature work.
  4. ShipDeliver a focused milestone, test the change, and document what matters.
