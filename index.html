Engineering notes

Real problems, solved in production.

Short write-ups of things I actually fixed and shipped — how I think, not just what I claim.

Reliability

Cutting a React Native crash rate from 2.90% to 1.67%

Most crashes traced to a handful of null-safety gaps and native-module cleanup that had drifted after upgrades. I triaged the top Crashlytics clusters by impacted-user count (not raw volume), fixed the highest-leverage ones first, and guarded the JS–native bridge where undefined data crossed it.

React Native

Migrating custom native modules to TurboModules incrementally

Upgrading across 15 minor versions meant moving custom native modules to TurboModules on iOS and Android — one module at a time behind the interop layer, so releases never stopped while the architecture changed underneath.

Security

Taking a banking app through penetration testing with zero criticals left

Hardened a regulated banking app with certificate pinning, biometrics/MFA, root/jailbreak detection, anti-tampering and encryption at rest, then worked with the pen-test team to remediate every finding before store submission — shipped with no open critical or high issues.

Connected hardware

Getting BLE reconnect above 99% on a device companion app

A Bluetooth device kept dropping its link when the app backgrounded. Moving re-subscription onto the connection-state callback (instead of component mount) and adding a bounded retry took reconnect success from flaky to consistently above 99% in the field.

Performance

Shrinking a React Native bundle 15% without cutting features

An 89MB build was mostly leftover native artifacts and unoptimized images after several upgrades. Removing stale architecture outputs, enabling Hermes and Proguard/R8 shrinking, and compressing assets took it to 76MB — and faster cold starts as a bonus.

CI/CD

Automating releases cut turnaround from ~2 days to ~2 hours

Replaced a manual, error-prone release with Fastlane — automated code signing, staged rollouts and release-health checks on iOS and Android. What took a couple of days of hand-holding now runs in about two hours, with fewer mistakes.
